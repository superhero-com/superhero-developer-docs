# Claiming Rewards

Three different things on Superhero are called "claiming a reward". One is automatic, one is
a contract call you sign, and one is signed by a one-time key embedded in an invite link.

## The three payout paths

| Reward | How it reaches you | Who signs | Gas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X posting rewards | Sent automatically to your connected wallet after a successful scan | Nobody: the backend pays out | None for you |
| Affiliate rewards | You press **Collect rewards**, which calls `withdraw()` on the AffiliationTreasury | You | An ordinary æternity transaction fee |
| An invite link's AE gift | You open the link and press **Claim reward** | The invite's one-time key, not your wallet | Covered by the `redemption_fee_cover` the inviter funded |

## X posting rewards: paid automatically

The [Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program) pays out without a claim step.
Press **Check rewards** on `/trends/invite`, sign the ownership challenge with your wallet,
and the backend scans your posts and sends AE to your connected wallet. You sign no
transaction and pay no gas.

Checking your status:

- **The button shows a countdown.** You can run one scan per 24 hours. The status response
  carries `next_check_allowed_at`, and a submit before that time returns HTTP 429. Wait for
  the countdown, then check again.
- **The check completes with an `error` string.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 may explain
  why no reward was sent: the account has fewer than 100 followers, the X identity has already
  been rewarded on a different address, or the payout transaction did not complete. The card
  shows that string in a red banner; address the cause it names, then check again.
- **You dismissed the signature request.** Your state is unchanged. Press the button again and
  approve the signature.

## Affiliate rewards: the withdraw call

Affiliate rewards accrue in the `AffiliationTreasury` contract as your invitees trade.

**The gate:** four direct invitees must each have spent at least 10 AE, cumulatively, in
token sales. The Collect Rewards card reads this live from the contract, shows a progress bar
out of four, and lists each invitee's spend against the 10 AE threshold.

**The claim window:** rewards accumulate in 30-day periods. `withdraw()` pays the current and
previous period, so a given reward is claimable for between 30 and 60 days depending on where
in a period it landed. Withdraw at least monthly. The arithmetic is on
[The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation).

To withdraw:

1. Open `https://superhero.com/trends/invite` and connect the wallet that created the
   invites. Rewards accrue to the inviter address.
2. Scroll to **Collect your rewards**. The card shows **Available Rewards**, the value of
   `get_accumulated_rewards(<your address>)`, converted from aettos.
3. If the progress bar reads 4/4 and the balance is above zero, the button is enabled. Press
   **Collect rewards** and confirm the transaction in your wallet. It calls `withdraw()`.

| Button reads | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not eligible yet | The gate opens once four direct invitees have each crossed 10 AE; the bar shows progress |
| No rewards yet | Threshold reached; `get_accumulated_rewards` returns zero so far |
| Collect rewards | Eligible; the call will go through |
| Withdrawing... | Transaction submitted, waiting on the wallet and the chain |

`withdraw()` takes no arguments and pays the whole two-period balance in one call. Its one
requirement is the `ThresholdReached` check. The call pays gas at the network's current gas
price, so wait for the card to show a balance above zero before pressing the button.

Contract messages the card translates:

- `MINIMUM_ACCOUNTS_THRESHOLD_NOT_REACHED` and `MINIMUM_OF_4_ACCOUNTS_SHALL_BUY` both render
  as "you need 4 direct invitees who each spent at least 10 AE in token sales". If you see
  this while the bar shows 4/4, reload the page to refresh the on-chain state.
- A `Key does not exist` or `Maps:` message from `get_unique_invitee` means the contract has
  no entry for your address yet. The card treats that as zero progress. It is the normal state
  for an account that has not invited anyone yet.

## An invite link's AE gift: signed by the link's one-time key

When someone opens an invite link, the app reads `invite_code` out of the URL fragment,
reconstructs a one-time account from the secret it contains, and shows a claim card with the
funded amount and who sent it.

Pressing **Claim reward** calls `redeem_invitation_code(<your wallet address>)` **signed by
the invite's one-time key**. The inviter funds a `redemption_fee_cover` alongside the gift so
the one-time account can pay for its own redemption transaction. Your wallet is connected so
the contract knows where to send the AE and whom to attach to the affiliation tree.

| Message | What happened and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| `ALREADY_REDEEMED` | The link was already claimed. The card also detects this up front by reading the third field of `get_invitation_code`. Ask the inviter for a fresh link. |
| `INVITEE_ALREADY_REGISTERED` | Your wallet address is already attached to an inviter. Each account is invited once. |
| `INVITER_EQUAL_INVITEE` | You are redeeming your own code into your own wallet. Share the link with someone else instead. |
| `Trying to call undefined function` | Your wallet is set to a different network from the treasury. Switch networks and retry. |
| A message mentioning `Secret key` | The link was cut short or altered in transit. Ask the inviter for the full link. |

If you are the **inviter** and you open your own link, the card switches to **Revoke
invitation** instead of Claim, and calls `revoke_invitation_code` with your wallet. Revoking
returns the `invitation_amount` to you. An unclaimed invite link stays claimable until you
revoke it.
