# Superhero Rewards Program

The Superhero Rewards Program pays AE for promoting Superhero on X. You link your X account
to your on-chain SuperheroID, post about Superhero, and the backend scans your posts and sends
AE to your connected wallet.

The affiliate program is separate: affiliate rewards come from trading fees and are
withdrawn from a contract. See [The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation).

## Where it lives

The program is at the top of `https://superhero.com/trends/invite`, above the Refer & Earn
cards. Connect your wallet first so the page can track your progress.

## Milestone 1: Link X Account & Post

**Reward: 50 AE.** Two steps, both of which must complete before milestone 2 opens.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Link your X account to your on-chain SuperheroID. The button sends you to your profile page at `/users/<your address>`. |
| 2 | Post about Superhero from that linked account. The post must mention Superhero: either a `superhero.com` link or a tag of `@superhero_chain`. |

The app pre-fills the compose intent with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! https://superhero.com
```

- **Accounts with at least 100 followers qualify.**
- Milestone 1 is a **regular post**; your referral link comes into play at milestone 2.

Linking X also sets up your SuperheroID profile. See [SuperheroID](/identity/superhero-id)
and [X verification](/identity/x-verification).

## Milestone 2: Post on X & Earn

**Reward: 10–30 AE per qualifying post, plus a 50 AE streak bonus.** Opens once milestone 1
is paid.

Every post must contain **your unique referral link** in the post text. The link is minted
for you the first time you press "Post on X" on the rewards card; the app fetches it, then
opens an X compose intent pre-filled with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! <your referral link>
```

Accounts with at least 100 followers qualify, and your per-post rate is set by your follower
count:

| Follower count | Per qualifying post |
| --- | --- |
| 100 – 10,000 | 10 AE |
| 10,000 – 1,000,000 | 20 AE |
| Over 1,000,000 | 30 AE |

Two counters run on this milestone:

- **Posts rewarded**: the card tracks progress toward **10 rewarded posts** and reads
  "completed" at 10.
- **Streak**: posting on 10 consecutive days earns an **extra 50 AE**. The card shows your
  current streak out of 10 days. The bonus is recurring: when the streak reaches 10 it pays
  out and the counter resets, so the next streak starts from zero.

At the base tier, milestone 1 plus 10 posts plus one streak bonus adds up to 200 AE:
50 AE + 10 × 10 AE + 50 AE. Earnings continue past that at one rewarded post per UTC day.

## The three payout paths

| Path | Trigger | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Your first post containing `superhero.com` or `superhero_chain` | One-time |
| Per-post | A post containing your referral link | Once per UTC day, at your follower tier's rate |
| Streak bonus | 10 consecutive UTC days of referral posts | Recurring: the counter resets and starts again |

Qualifying posts, per-post rewards and streak progress accrue to accounts with at least 100
followers. Payouts already accrued always settle.

### What counts as a qualifying post

- **Referral posts are matched on the host.** A URL in the post must have host `superhero.com`
  and a `?ref=` query parameter equal to your referral code, case-insensitively.
- **One rewarded post per UTC day.** The first qualifying referral post of each UTC day is
  rewarded.
- **A streak day is a UTC day.** The streak grows by one when a qualifying day directly follows
  the last qualifying day; after a gap it starts again at 1. Days are counted from UTC
  midnight, so plan your posts by UTC rather than local time.
- **A scan covers your 2,000 newest tweets.** Post and check regularly so each qualifying post
  falls inside that window. The status response carries `x_posts_scan_truncated` when a scan
  reached that limit.
- **Payouts are final.** Rewarded AE is sent on-chain and stays yours. A rewarded post that is
  later deleted simply stops appearing in future scans.

## How verification works

The payout is the on-chain step. The flow is a signed request to the Superhero backend, which
scans X on your behalf.

1. The rewards card calls `GET /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward` on load and renders
   your status, rewarded post count, streak, follower count, tier and referral link.
2. Pressing **Check rewards** requests a challenge from
   `POST /api/profile/x-posting-reward/recheck-challenge`. The response carries a `nonce`,
   an expiry and a `message`.
3. Your wallet signs that message. The signature, nonce and expiry are submitted to
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward/recheck`, which runs the scan and returns
   your updated status.
4. Minting the referral link uses the same signed proof against
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-reward/referral-link`.

**You can run one scan per 24 hours.** The status response carries `next_check_allowed_at`;
the button shows a countdown until that time, and a submit before it returns HTTP 429. A
pending payout from an earlier scan is settled on your next recheck, including during the
cooldown, because settling it needs no new scan.

**Read the status after a check.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 may carry an `error` string
when no reward was sent: the account has fewer than 100 followers, the X identity has already
been rewarded on another address, or the payout transaction did not complete. The card shows
that string as a red banner, so you know what to change before the next check.

**Payouts are automatic.** AE is sent to your connected wallet by the backend, with no
transaction to sign and no gas to pay. See [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming) for the
rewards you collect with a transaction.

## Eligibility

The program runs under the terms shown at the foot of the rewards page: it is discretionary,
Superhero may pause it, and it is open to users outside restricted regions. Rewards may be
reportable as income.
