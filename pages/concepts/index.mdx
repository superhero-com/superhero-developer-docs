# Core Concepts

Superhero combines on-chain posts, community tokens priced by bonding curves, and token-holder
governance. These pages explain the contracts and services behind those features, which data
is stored on chain, and how trading fees reach community treasuries and referrers.

If you already know what a bonding curve is, skip ahead to
[Architecture Overview](/concepts/architecture) or [Where the Money Goes](/concepts/value-flow).

## Three mechanisms

Superhero runs on three mechanisms. Most questions about the product come back to one of them.

**Posts are contract calls.** A post is a `post_without_tip` call on the `Tipping.aes` v3 contract.
The author's address, the text and a list of media URLs are written into contract state and indexed
by a `tip_id`. Nobody, including Superhero, can delete it afterwards.

**Topics are tokens.** Any hashtag can be turned into an AEX-9 token whose price is a deterministic
function of its own circulating supply. There is no order book and no counterparty: you buy from the
curve and sell back to it, using a reserve funded by buys. See
[Reserve accounting and rounding](/concepts/value-flow#reserve-accounting-and-rounding) for the
continuous-model redemption argument and its implementation limits.

**The spread funds the community.** The spread is 1% of curve value, including the affiliation
fee added on buys. On each buy, half is allocated to the token's own DAO treasury and half to a
four-level referral chain, before integer rounding. Token holders vote on what the treasury does
with it.
