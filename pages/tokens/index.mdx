# Tokens & Bonding Curves

Anyone can turn a topic into a market. Type a name, and once the deployment is mined that idea has a token,
a price and a treasury, with no listing, approval or market maker involved.

The price comes from a curve rather than an order book. Every buy mints new tokens and nudges the
price up; every sell burns them and moves it back down. You trade with the contract itself,
which computes quotes from supply and retains AE from buys to pay sellers. See
[Reserve accounting and rounding](/concepts/value-flow#reserve-accounting-and-rounding) for the
continuous-model redemption argument and its implementation limits. Tokens bought before the
supply grows cost less.

## The numbers to remember

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Curve | `buy(x) = 0.001 · e^(0.00000001 · x) − 0.0009999`, in AE per token |
| Sell curve | `sell(x) = buy(x) · 0.995` |
| Price of a trade | The integral of the curve across the supply you mint into or burn out of |
| Launch price | 0.0000001 AE per token, at supply 0 |
| Spread | 1% of the curve value: 0.5% to the community DAO, 0.5% to affiliation |
| Instant round trip | Returns about 0.99005 of what you paid, before gas |
| Affiliation split | 0.300% / 0.125% / 0.050% / 0.025% of curve value across four referral levels; uncredited shares stay in the affiliation treasury |
| ProtocolDAO reward | Minted to the buyer on every buy, proportional to its AE value |
| Token standard | AEX-9, 18 decimals |
| Reserve design | Buys retain the immediate sell quote; full redemption follows in the continuous model, subject to integer-rounding analysis |
