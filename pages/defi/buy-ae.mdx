# Buying AE

AE is the native coin of the æternity blockchain. You need it to pay transaction fees, buy
community tokens on the bonding curve, swap on the DEX, and fund invite links.

The Get AE page at `https://superhero.com/get-ae` collects every route: ways to earn AE inside
Superhero, and exchanges and swap aggregators where you can buy it. The older `/buy-ae` path
redirects there.

## Earn AE

| Route | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Refer & Earn | [`/trends/invite`](https://superhero.com/trends/invite); see [Invite Links](/rewards/invite-links) |
| Earn by posting on X | The [Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program), from the Refer & Earn page |

Refer & Earn needs some AE to start with: each invite link is funded with the gift amount plus a
0.001 AE fee cover for the invitee's redemption. It grows a balance you already have.

## Buy AE

The Get AE page links to this provider:

| Provider | What it is | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate.io | Centralised exchange with an AE/USDT market | `https://www.gate.io/trade/AE_USDT` |

If you already hold ETH, bridge it to æternity as aeETH and swap it for AE on the Superhero DEX. See
[Bridging from Ethereum](/defi/bridge).

Identity verification, payment methods, purchase limits and country availability are set by the
provider. Gate.io requires an account and identity verification. Superhero collects no payment
information.

## Before you buy

- **Set up a wallet first** so you have an `ak_` address to receive at. See
  [Fund your wallet](/getting-started/fund-wallet).
- **Confirm the token, the network, the deposit address, the withdrawal fee and the minimum
  withdrawal.** AE also exists as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum; Superhero uses native AE on
  æternity, so withdraw to the æternity network.
- **Start with a small test transaction** before moving larger amounts.
- **Keep custody in your own wallet**, and never share private keys or seed phrases with anyone,
  including support staff.
- **Check availability.** Pairs, routes and supported regions change; confirm details with the
  provider before sending funds.
