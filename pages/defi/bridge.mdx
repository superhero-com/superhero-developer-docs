# Bridging from Ethereum

Bridging runs through æternity's own Ethereum bridge. Inside Superhero, `/defi/bridge` and
`/defi/buy-ae-with-eth` take you to `/defi/swap`, where bridged aeETH trades.

## aeETH on the DEX

aeETH is the AEX-9 representation of bridged ETH on æternity. It trades on the DEX: if you hold
aeETH, swap it at [`/defi/swap`](/defi/swap) like any other token, typically through the aeETH/WAE
pair.

| Network | aeETH contract |
| --- | --- |
| Mainnet | `ct_ryTY1mxqjCjq1yBn9i6HDaCSdA6thXUFZTA84EMzbWd1SLKdh` |
| Testnet | `ct_WVqAvLQpvZCgBg4faZLXA1YBj43Fxj91D33Z8K7pFsY8YCofv` |

aeETH represents ETH held by the bridge on Ethereum. [WAE](/defi/wrapped-ae) is backed by AE held
in one contract on æternity.

## Moving ETH to æternity

æternity operates its own Ethereum bridge, documented on the
[æternity docs hub](https://docs.aeternity.com/aeternity-user-tools-and-services/aerc-bridge).
Bridge ETH to aeETH there, then swap it for AE on the DEX. To buy AE directly, use an exchange
listed under [Buying AE](/defi/buy-ae).
