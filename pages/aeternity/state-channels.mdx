# State Channels

State channels are æternity's layer-2 scaling mechanism: a private, two-party ledger that settles
on chain only at its endpoints. Superhero's posts, tips, trades and votes are individual
on-chain transactions, so each one is public and verifiable; chat runs on Nostr relays and video
calls on Jitsi. Channels are available on the same chain for two-party, high-frequency flows of
your own.

## How a channel works

**Opening** is an on-chain transaction. Both participants lock funds into the channel and agree on
an initial state, each authenticating it with a signature. From then on they exchange updates
directly with each other (a transfer, a contract call, any agreed change), each co-signed by
both parties and given a round number. None of it touches the chain, so none of it costs a fee
or waits for a block.

**Closing** is on chain again. In the happy path the participants co-sign a final state and
settle mutually. If one party disappears or misbehaves, the other can close unilaterally with
`channel_solo_close_tx`, publishing the state they claim is current. If a party publishes an
*old* state, the counterparty answers with `channel_slash_tx`, supplying a co-signed state with a
higher round number, and `channel_settle` then pays out. The honest party always holds a newer
co-signed state than a cheater can produce, so cheating is unprofitable.

The economics: two on-chain transactions, amortised over as many off-chain updates as the
participants make. The more updates a channel carries, the better the amortisation.

## Superhero and channels

Channels fit two-party, high-frequency interaction. A public social feed is a different shape: a
post is visible to everyone, and trading against a bonding curve is many-party, so Superhero puts
each of those on chain individually. [Delegated posting](/social/delegated-posting) lets a user
sign a message off chain while a relayer pays for the transaction, and the post lands on chain
with the signer as its author.

If you need state channels on æternity, they are fully supported at the protocol and SDK level
and run alongside Superhero's contracts on the same chain.
